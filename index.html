Intellectual Properties

Five Patent Claims, No prior art found globally.

Filed at the Indian Patent Office, Chennai.


Carrier-Launch UAV Architecture

A miniature UAV is carried, transported and launched from a fixed-wing response aircraft near the incident location.


GPS-Anchored Tower Dump Filtration

GPS-anchored tower data is filtered and narrowed to identify relevant devices and locations within the incident zone.


Indoor BLE-Wi-Fi-BMS Safety Response

BLE, Wi-Fi and building-management signals work together to support safety response in indoor environments and blind spots.


Offline Graceful Degradation

Safety functions continue locally and degrade gracefully when connectivity is unavailable, preserving essential response capability.


UAV Swarm Mesh Network

Multiple UAVs can communicate as a resilient mesh network, extending coverage and maintaining coordinated aerial response.
